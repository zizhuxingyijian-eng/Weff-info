About the Event

What is WEFF Showcase Forum?

The WEFF Showcase Forum is our flagship annual event — a platform for external guests, alumni, and partner organisations to see the work our members do in economics, finance, and investment.

🎤

External Showcase

A platform for WEFF members to present research, analysis, and investment pitches to an external audience of professionals and alumni.

🤝

Networking & Branding

Structured networking with external guests, alumni judges, and partner organisations to build meaningful professional connections.

🎯

Joint Club Effort

A shared milestone for all WEFF members — from Investment and B&E teams to PR & Comms — working together towards one event.

Event Program

What to Expect

Three main segments make up the Showcase Forum afternoon.

B&E Symposium
  • 2–3 student research presentations
  • Keynote address by an industry guest
  • Open Q&A and panel discussion
Led by WEFF's Business & Economics team.
Open to all attendees.
Pitch Competition
  • 4 pitches total — 2 from WEFF, 2 from KUTIC
  • Judged by a panel of 4 alumni
  • Live feedback and Q&A after each pitch
Led by WEFF's Investment team.
Investment & finance focus.
Networking Break
  • Structured networking for all participants
  • Meet WEFF members, alumni, and guests
  • Refreshments provided
Led by PR & Comms team.
All participants welcome.
