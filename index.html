About the Event

What is WEFF Showcase Forum?

The Waseda Economic and Finance Forum 2026 is an English-language event bringing together student presenters, expert guests, and university students around Tokyo to address the most pressing economic trends affecting young people today. As the inaugural edition of the forum format, it is an intimate touchpoint for convening members past and present.

🎤

Symposium & Pitches

A day-long forum comprising a symposium on macroeconomic and business insights, followed by a stock pitch competition.

🎓

Career Insight

For lower undergraduate students, a valuable opportunity to gain insight into finance and economics career pathways and network with alumni in these fields.

2026 Theme

Reflation Generation

For decades, Japan was defined by caution: cash savings, low inflation, and conservative corporate balance sheets. This landscape has changed. Policymakers are attempting a historic shift toward investment-driven growth, urging households and companies to put idle cash to work through initiatives like expanded NISA programs and corporate governance reforms from the Tokyo Stock Exchange. Yet the same forces driving opportunity — inflation, global competition, and market transformation — also generate anxiety. The Waseda Economic and Finance Forum Summer 2026 explores whether Japan can truly transform its vast cash reserves into productive growth amid uncertainty, and whether the "reflation generation" is prepared to ride the wave or be overwhelmed by it.

Pitch Competition Theme

Capital Efficiency in a Changing Market

With rising costs, economic uncertainty, and rapid technological change, companies face growing pressure to justify investment decisions and protect profitability. This theme explores how companies deploy capital, evaluate investment decisions, and generate sustainable returns in an evolving global market. Teams will select a company, conduct an in-depth analysis of its capital structure, and deliver a stock pitch following standard pitch format.
