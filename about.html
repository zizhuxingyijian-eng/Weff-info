Our Mission

Who We Are

Waseda Economics and Finance Forum (WEFF) is the premier economics and finance organisation for international students of Waseda University.

The primary mission of WEFF is to promote the importance of economics and finance by providing a series of venues for members to discuss and engage in issues related to these fields.

We offer exclusive lectures, career support, and market updates — and we're open to students from all Japanese universities, not just Waseda.
